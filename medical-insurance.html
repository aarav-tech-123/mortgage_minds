Private Surgery & Hospital Care

Medical insurance assists in the coverage of the private surgeries, hospitalization, consultation with the specialists, tests and authorized follow-up treatment in case of necessity.

Serious Illness Treatment

The cover is applicable in chemotherapy, radiotherapy, immunotherapy and other approved therapies to treat serious illnesses and to help in recovering.

Major Diagnostic Tests

The policies can cover MRI scans, CT scans, PET scans, colonoscopies, angiograms and other significant medical tests.

Travel & Accommodation Support

Covers the cost of travel, accommodation, and support-person expenses in the event of an out-of-town treatment.

Children’s Cover

Children’s cover can help protect dependent children and may include support for eligible pre-existing medical conditions.

Extra Peace-of-Mind Benefits

Additional benefits may include second medical opinions, home nursing services, rehabilitation and funeral assistance depending on the policy one chooses.
