Total Disability Benefit

Offers monthly financial support if you are sick or injured and are unable to work and earn a steady income for a long period of time.

Partial Disability Benefit

Helps provide partial income support if you return to work with reduced hours or lower earnings during recovery.

Life Events Benefit

Allows you to increase your property mortgage insurance cover after major life changes like buying a home or increasing your mortgage.

Recovery Support Benefit

Can assist with expenses for mobility equipment, home modifications, rehabilitation or recovery-related support after disability.

Return to Work Benefit

Provides additional financial assistance when transitioning back into full-time employment after a claim period.

Severe Illness Benefit

Offers a one-off payment if diagnosed with a serious covered illness to help manage treatment and immediate expenses.
