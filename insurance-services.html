Protect Your Future With the Right Insurance Cover

Life is full of surprises, but the correct insurance cover may help protect you and your family through the tough times. At Mortgage Minds we assist you find insurance policies that suit your lifestyle, budget and future needs so you can feel more comfortable and prepared.

Life Insurance

Life Insurance pays your family if you pass away or are suffering from a terminal illness. It helps reduce financial stress in tough times.

Trauma Recovery Cover

If you’re diagnosed with a serious medical condition covered under your policy, Trauma Cover will pay you a lump sum amount. It will help cover medical costs and supports recovery.

Medical Insurance

Medical Insurance helps pay the costs of private medical treatment, specialized care and hospital costs so you may obtain healthcare faster when you need it.

Mortgage & Rent Protection Cover

This coverage helps you manage mortgage or rent payments if you are temporarily unable to work and earn income due to illness or injury.

Total Permanent Disability Benefit Cover

TPD Cover is a lump sum payment if you become seriously disabled and can’t work permanently.

Income Protection Cover

Income Protection Cover can assist replace part of your income if you cannot work due to illness or injury, so you can manage regular expenses.
